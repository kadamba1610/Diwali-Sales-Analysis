## 📌 Project Overview

This project performs **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** on a Diwali Sales dataset to uncover customer purchasing patterns, sales trends, and business insights.

The analysis helps businesses understand:
- 🎯 Who their most valuable customers are
- 🛍️ Which product categories generate the most sales
- 🌍 Which states contribute the highest revenue
- 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Customer demographics and buying behavior

---

## 🎯 Objectives

✅ Clean and preprocess the dataset

✅ Handle missing values

✅ Perform demographic analysis

✅ Identify top-performing states

✅ Analyze sales based on:
- 👩 Gender
- 🎂 Age Group
- 💍 Marital Status
- 💼 Occupation
- 🛒 Product Category

✅ Generate actionable business insights

## 📊 Exploratory Data Analysis

### 🚻 Gender Analysis
- Compared purchase amounts by gender
- Identified major contributors to revenue

### 🎂 Age Group Analysis
- Studied purchasing trends across age groups
- Determined the most active customer segments

### 🌍 State Analysis
- Compared sales and orders by state
- Identified top revenue-generating regions

### 💼 Occupation Analysis
- Evaluated spending behavior across professions
- Found occupations contributing highest sales

### 🛒 Product Category Analysis
- Identified best-selling categories
- Compared order volume and revenue contribution

---

## 🔑 Key Insights

✨ Female customers contribute significantly to total sales.

✨ Customers aged **26–35 years** represent a major purchasing segment.

✨ Certain states generate substantially higher revenue than others.

✨ Occupation influences customer spending behavior.

✨ Product categories vary significantly in sales performance.
